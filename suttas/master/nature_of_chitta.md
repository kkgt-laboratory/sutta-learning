# Nature of Chitta

The nature of the chitta is multi-fold. It encapsulates all the 8 Steps of the Noble Path in a luminous, clear, compassionate and open way.
On seeing the nature of the chitta, we understand that Nibbana is simply the state where all actions of the mind flow through the light
of open and aware consciousness, rather than evil and darkness. This makes us comfortable in the abiding of the Dhamma, and we become
compassionate towards ourselves, and all sentient beings dressed up as a direct reminder in our mindful awakening.

When we first experience the chitta, we feel like we have always experienced it. The chitta is impermanent, just like all sensory arisings.
The open light of awareness in the chitta, burns impurities such as defilements primarily prejedice, guilty, lackture, and torment. As we
continue to experience the chitta through consistent and diligent meditation, our behaviour becomes refined, and our action becomes effective.
Love becomes an embodied and continuous outpouring of the sensory and unsensory arisings, and we don't need to memorize dependent origination
because it becomes an automatic aspect of our everyday life.

The further we move towards the light of the chitta, we see that even darkness has a light that the chitta activates. The chitta is not limited
to the luminous mind alone, but encomprises of all the 1182 factors of enlightenment that we study in the Noble 12 Fold Path starting from
right understanding, to right unification. In between, every step requires the levelling of right concentration so that it does not
incorrectly permeate consciousness and damage the roots of life. As we undertake the journey of freeing ourselves from suffering, we notice
that the chitta boosts mindfulness, and supports concentration to assist the higher states of enlightenment especially Jhanic reasoning.

Jhana is one of the ways in which the chitta can be refined. With unification, we don't just refine our chitta, but we refine "the chitta",
which is a global, unified, complete, and all-encompassing nature of a conscious spirit and life force that embalms the Buddha's True Nature
in accordance with our True Nature to completely capture the 3 marks of existence; impermanence, no self, and unsatisfactoriness (non grasping).
The further more the chitta developers, the further more the grasping of unwholesome tendencies reaches cessation and the mind becomes free from
corruption, greed, hatred, agony, anger, lust, and melancholy. Hence, only when we support all the 114 aspects of wholesome enlightenment,
we can truly experience the chitta and attain as well as maintain Nibbana and it's higher states of being.
